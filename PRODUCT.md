# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Curious learners, space enthusiasts, and web users exploring the Solar System through focused, interactive 3D scenes. They want to inspect each celestial body directly, understand its distinctive visual character, and move fluidly between bodies without needing specialist controls.

## Product Purpose

Solar System Explorer makes the Sun, planets, the Moon, and Pluto tangible through responsive Three.js scenes with realistic textures, lighting, atmospheres, axial tilts, and direct manipulation. Success means each body is immediately recognizable, rewarding to examine, and smooth enough to explore on a modern desktop or mobile device.

## Brand Personality

Scientific, immersive, restrained. The experience should evoke curiosity and quiet awe while keeping the interface secondary to the celestial body.

## Anti-references

Avoid arcade-like space effects, cinematic lens-flare spectacle, strong bloom, hard or perfectly repeated light rays, uniform circular halos, decorative UI motion, and controls that compete with the object being explored.

## Design Principles

- Let the celestial body remain the primary interface and visual focus.
- Use physically suggestive detail to improve recognition without pretending to be a full simulation.
- Favor subtle layered effects over a single conspicuous treatment.
- Keep controls familiar, compact, and directly tied to visible behavior.
- Preserve responsive performance and graceful degradation across capable browsers and devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain clear accessible labels for interactive scenes and navigation, keyboard-readable controls through established components, sufficient UI contrast, and reduced-motion behavior for nonessential animation. Avoid relying on color alone to communicate state.
